No account. Your study data stays on your phone.

  • No sign-up or login. Open the app and start studying.
  • Decks, bookmarks, scores, and your streak are saved only on your phone. They are not uploaded to any AralAI server.
  • Only your topic or document is sent to the AI (Google Gemini) so it can write your questions.
  • No ads.
  • No cloud backup yet. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes your decks and progress.

Questions

Is AralAI free?

Yes, completely free, with no ads.

Do I need an account?

No. Install it and start studying.

What files can I upload?

PDF and Word (.docx) files up to 10 MB. Files with real text (not just scanned photos) give the best results.

Will the questions really come from my handout?

Yes. When you upload a document, the AI is told to create questions only from that document.

How long does it take to make a quiz?

Usually 10 to 20 seconds for about 15 questions. Bigger quizzes, up to 50 questions, take a bit longer.

Can I use it offline?

You need internet to make a new quiz. Your saved decks, flashcards, bookmarks, and progress work without internet.

Is the AI always correct?

AralAI runs quality checks and asks the AI to double-check every answer, but AI can still make mistakes. If something looks off, compare it with your handout or ask your teacher.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. AralAI is available for Android only for now.

Where is my data saved?

On your phone only. Uninstalling the app removes it.
