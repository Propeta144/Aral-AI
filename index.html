Privacy & Local Storage

Aral AI works entirely without user accounts. Your generated quizzes, bookmarked questions, and progress analytics are stored locally on your device.

Note: Data remains saved when reopening the app, but clearing app storage or uninstalling will erase local decks as there is currently no cloud sync.

Project Direction

Aral AI is designed to become an adaptive personal study companion. The project focuses on providing an enjoyable flashcard experience, flexible AI quiz generation, crystal-clear answer explanations, and frictionless progress tracking without signup barriers.
